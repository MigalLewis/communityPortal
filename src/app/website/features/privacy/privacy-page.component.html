Privacy Policy

How the Parktown North Ratepayers' and Residents' Association (PNRA) collects, uses, protects and shares personal information.

Effective date: 1 September 2026 • Last revised: 1 September 2026

1. Who we are

PNRA is a voluntary community association serving Parktown North. For purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), PNRA is the responsible party for personal information processed through this website, membership administration and its community activities.

2. Information we collect

We may collect names, contact details, property or business details, membership and payment records, correspondence, service requests, event registrations and the technical information normally produced when you use a website. We only ask for information that is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

3. Why we process information

We process information to administer membership; respond to enquiries; deliver resident, safety and community services; communicate notices and events; manage payments and records; meet legal obligations; and protect PNRA, its members and the public. We rely on consent where required and otherwise process information where permitted by POPIA.

4. Sharing and operators

PNRA does not sell personal information. We may share it with authorised committee members, contracted service providers acting as operators, payment or technology providers, professional advisers and public authorities where necessary or legally required. Operators must protect the information and may use it only for the agreed service.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and the risk. No internet service is completely secure. We retain information only for as long as it is needed for its purpose, PNRA's legitimate record-keeping needs, or a legal requirement, and then securely delete or de-identify it.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to POPIA, you may ask whether we hold your information, request access or correction, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or request deletion where retention is no longer lawful. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

7. Cookies and external services

Our website may use essential browser storage and service providers needed to operate securely. Links to third-party websites are governed by those parties' privacy notices; PNRA is not responsible for their practices.

8. Children

Our services are not directed at children. A parent, guardian or other competent person must provide any required authorisation before personal information about a child is submitted.

9. Contact and complaints

For privacy requests or questions, email parktownnorthra@gmail.com or call 073 633 4560. If we cannot resolve a concern, you may lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator.

10. Changes to this policy

PNRA may update this policy when its practices or legal duties change. The revision date above identifies the version currently approved by PNRA.
