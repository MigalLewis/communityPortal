About PNRA

Protecting the character and future of Parktown North.

The Parktown North Ratepayers' and Residents' Association is a non-profit volunteer organisation dedicated to preserving the unique heritage, natural beauty, and vibrant community spirit of our historic suburb.

◉

Our Vision

To cultivate a vibrant, safe, and beautiful neighbourhood where community thrives, heritage is respected, and the natural environment is protected for generations to come.

Community in action

Working Together for Parktown North

The strength of Parktown North lies in the active participation of its residents. The PNRA serves as the central hub, facilitating collaboration between homeowners, local businesses, volunteers, and municipal authorities.

Whether it is organising community clean-ups, coordinating security initiatives, or hosting neighbourhood events, our collective efforts ensure that our suburb remains a premier place to live, work, and play.

The Suburb of Parktown North

Established in 1903, Parktown North is one of Johannesburg's oldest and most distinguished northern suburbs. Known for its historical homes, lush tree-lined avenues, and strong sense of community, it offers a unique blend of heritage charm and modern lifestyle.
